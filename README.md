# Hi, I am Luc Urlings

I am a Software Development Engineer at Amazon, based in Dublin. I like getting close to real problems, understanding the people affected by them, and building reliable software that gets used.

Good engineering is not defined by a fixed technology stack. I care about ownership, working well with different people, and choosing tools based on the problem rather than habit.

## What interests me

I enjoy understanding systems from first principles, from cloud infrastructure and self-hosting to building neural networks from scratch. GitHub is where I keep the projects and experiments that come out of that curiosity. More context about selected work is available on my [personal website](https://lucurlings.nl).

## Beyond software

I once left engineering for a season to become a ski instructor in British Columbia. Spending every day outdoors and teaching people a new skill remains one of the best things I have done.
